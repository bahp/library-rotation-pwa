Book Rotation User Guide & Overview

Book Rotation is a privacy-first, offline-capable Progressive Web App (PWA) built for families to catalog children’s books, track shared or per-child ownership, plan fresh shelf rotations with automatic cooldowns, and turn reading into active play.

1. Installing on iPhone, iPad, or Android

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Open the app in Safari → tap Share → tap Add to Home Screen. If you updated from an older build and see a cached status bar style, remove the icon from your Home Screen once and re-add it so iOS refreshes viewport-fit=cover and black-translucent edge-to-edge mode.
  • Android / Desktop Chrome: Open Settings (gear icon at top right) → tap Install App inside the Installable Phone App (PWA) card.
  • 100% Local Storage: Your books, custom cover photos, profiles, and rotation history are stored locally in IndexedDB on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you search an external book catalog or opt into an AI enrichment webhook.

2. Reader Profiles & Automatic Age Progression

Tap the active reader chip in the top-right header bar to switch children or add a new profile. When you enter a child’s Date of Birth:

  • Ages update automatically (in months for babies under 24 months, and in years thereafter).
  • Default reading stages (Board Book, Picture Book, Early Reader, Chapter Book) adapt to the child’s age and guide smart shelf rotation scoring.

3. Scanning & Adding Books

Open the center Scan tab to add books using three studio modes:

Intake Tab What It Does
Camera Tap directly on the viewfinder frame (or Start Camera) to stream your rear camera. Switch between three optical frame shapes: Barcode / QR (wide EAN-13 / ISBN-13 box), ISBN Text (narrow OCR strip for printed ISBN 978-... lines), and Cover (3:4 jacket frame with OCR title/ISBN extraction). Includes live Flashlight (rear torch) and Upload Photo fallback.
Search & ISBN Single smart search bar: type any book title, keyword, author, or paste a 10/13-digit ISBN. Checksum-verified ISBNs jump straight to exact edition lookup; text queries search enabled providers in parallel.
Manual Full form for handmade, vintage, or unlisted editions—complete with one-tap offline Suggest Topics from the title.

By default, scanned books save to Shared ownership as Owned so all siblings can enjoy them, or you can assign specific readers, mark a ♥ favorite, or save to your Wishlist right in the Found Book sheet.

4. Family Library, Filters & Book Inspector

  • Quick Status Icons & Filter Drawer: Filter instantaneously by All, Owned, Wishlist (bookmark icon), or Loved (heart icon), or open the slide-in Filters drawer to combine Format (Board Book, Paperback, Hardcover), Themes, Condition, and Multi-Reader Ownership.
  • Book Inspector Card: Tap any book to view its cover-tinted detail sheet, see whether metadata came from Google Books, Open Library, or both, replace cover art from camera/gallery/online search, log reads, or edit topics.

5. Shelf Rotations & Library Swap Day

Montessori and early-literacy research shows children engage far more deeply with 5–8 curated books on a forward-facing shelf than 80 crowded spines. In Rotations:

  • Smart Rotation Builder: Automatically scores owned books by age fit, reading stage, favorite topics, format variety, and rest cooldowns so recently shelved books rest before reappearing.
  • Library Swap Day Cadence: Choose a rotation cycle (e.g., every 7, 10, 14, 21, or 30 days). Both Home and Rotations show when the shelf was set, the target Swap Day date, a visual progress bar, and a gentle reminder banner when it is time to refresh the shelf.

6. Play Mode & Portable JSON Backups

  • Storytime Picker: Ends bedtime negotiation by randomly dealing 1–4 books from the Active Shelf (or Owned Library), optionally filtered by a bedtime topic such as bears, space, or winter.
  • Book Hunt ("Find It!"): Gives toddlers an energetic physical shelf treasure hunt clue (by cover color, topic, or title peek) to find and bring back from their real bookcase.
  • Export / Import JSON: In Settings → Backup & Portability, export your entire catalog and rotation history as a portable .json file anytime.
