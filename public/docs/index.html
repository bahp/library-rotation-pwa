2. Start in 3 Steps

1

Set Child’s Birth Date

Tap the child chip in the top-left header. Age in months automatically tunes rotation sizes and reading-stage fits.

2

Scan a Stack of Books

Open Scan, point at the back-cover ISBN barcode (`978…`), and tap Save Owned. Use Search for older books without barcodes.

3

Generate Your Shelf

Open Rotations → tap + (or Presets) to pick 5–8 age-matched books and place those physical books face-out.

3. Editing Shelves & Collection Presets

What you want to do How to do it
Rename a shelf or edit its description On Rotations, tap the pen icon (✎) right next to the shelf title. The title becomes a textbox and the description expands to show all lines. Press Save.
Swap, add, or remove books Tap Edit on a shelf card to reveal per-book Read, Random Swap, Manual Replace, and Remove buttons.
Use a seasonal or milestone preset Open Rotations → Presets (#/presets). Browse 15+ ready-made themes (Winter Holidays, Lunar New Year, Back to School, New Sibling, Calm Bedtime) with countdown badges like In 12 days.
Create your own preset In Presets, tap + Add Preset. Set topic tags, book count, swap cycle, cooldown, and an optional yearly start date (month + day).

4. Install on Your Phone (Offline PWA)

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Little Shelf in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with upward arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Little Shelf in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the in-app banner (or ⋮ → Install app).

5. Private Storage & Backups

100% On-Device Storage Books, covers, profiles, rotations, and custom presets stay in IndexedDB on your phone or laptop. Before switching phones or clearing browser data, open Settings → Export Backup (.json).
