2. Start in 3 Steps

1

Set Child’s Birth Date

Tap the top profile chip. Age in months automatically tunes rotation counts (5–12 books) and reading-stage heuristics.

2

Scan a Stack of Books

Open Scan, point at the back-cover barcode (978…), and tap Save Owned. Use Search for books without barcodes.

3

Generate Your Shelf

Open Rotations → tap + (or browse Presets) to pick 5–8 age-matched books and put them face-out on your physical shelf.

3. Editing Shelves & Collection Presets

What you want to do How to do it
Edit a shelf’s name, description, rules, or books On Rotations, tap Edit. Change the title, description, swap cycle, cooldown window, binding, or swap/remove books. Tap Done to save automatically.
Use a seasonal or milestone preset Open Rotations → Presets (#/presets). Browse 15+ curated themes (Winter Holidays, Lunar New Year, Back to School, New Sibling, Calm Bedtime) with automatic countdown badges.
Remove a rotation without losing book history Tap Delete Rotation. The books remain safely in your catalog, their past reading dates stay recorded, and the app gracefully rolls back the rotation counter.

4. Bedtime Baskets & Off-Shelf Book Sets

A common question from parents: "How do I create a set of books that are NOT on the daytime shelf? For example, all our bedtime stories are kept on a nightstand basket and only read before sleep."

Method A: Dedicated "Bedtime Set" Saved Rotation (Recommended)

  1. Go to Rotations (#/rotations) → Tap + New Shelf.
  2. Name it "Bedtime Tales" or "Night Basket".
  3. Add all your evening bedtime favorites to this shelf.
  4. Crucial Step: Do not tap "Make Active Shelf"! Leave it in Saved status.

Because it is a Saved Shelf, these books will never appear on the child’s daytime living room shelf (#/home) and will never clutter daytime play.

Method B: Night-Only Topic Tag (bedtime)

Tag your bedtime books with bedtime. When generating your daytime rotation shelf, set daytime objectives (e.g. animals, trucks, nature) so daytime auto-generation only selects daytime titles, keeping your bedtime books in reserve.

Why this works so well: You can keep multiple specialized off-shelf sets ready at the same time: a Bedtime Basket on the nightstand, a Car Travel Pouch in the glovebox, and a Holiday Box in storage!

5. Storytime Randomizer: Pick from Specific Shelves & Sets

In the Play tab (#/play), open the Bedtime & Storytime Randomizer. You can adjust the number of books with the − N books + stepper, toggle Unread first, and choose exactly where the books come from:

Scope Option How it picks When to use
Active Shelf Picks from the 5–10 books currently out on your child's main shelf. Everyday reading or when you want your child to focus on the current rotation.
Saved Shelf / Set Picks directly from any specific rotation you choose in the dropdown (e.g. "🌙 Bedtime Tales (6 books)"). Bedtime! When you want 2 or 3 surprise books picked strictly from the night basket, without daytime books interfering.
Home Library Draws from your reader's entire owned collection across all boxes. When looking for a book on a specific theme (e.g. typing "space" or "dinosaurs") anywhere in the house.

6. Book Cover Stickers & Badges (4-Corner Taxonomy)

To keep book cover artwork clear and beautiful, Little Shelf places status stickers in 4 dedicated, non-overlapping corner slots. Some badges are mutually exclusive (only the most important status wins), while others can appear simultaneously.

Top-Left: Catalog State (Mutually Exclusive)

Reserved exclusively for special catalog statuses: Wishlist, Loan, Saved, or Torn / Broken. Standard Owned + Good rotatable books leave this slot empty so artwork stays clean.

Top-Right: Affection (Independent Boolean)

Displays ♥ Loved for the child's comfort favorites. Can appear alongside any status or owner.

Bottom-Left: Rotation & Usage (Strict Hierarchy)

Displays live lifecycle: ✓ Read (solid green), ⏱ Shelf 12d (amber stale spotlight), ▤ Shelf (dark active), ⏱ Rest 15d (resting cooldown), or ✦ New (fresh addition).

Bottom-Right: Reader Owners (Multi-Child)

Shows child initials (e.g. M · L) when a book belongs to specific siblings rather than the shared family library.

7. Install on Your Phone (Offline PWA)

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Little Shelf in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen → tap Add.

Opens full-screen without Safari browser bars.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Little Shelf in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the in-app banner (or tap ⋮ → Install app).

Launches instantly like a native app from your home screen.

8. Private Storage & Backups

100% On-Device Storage: Books, photos, reader profiles, reading histories, and custom presets stay stored in IndexedDB on your device. Before clearing your browser cache or switching devices, go to Settings → Export Library Backup (.json) to save a complete copy.
